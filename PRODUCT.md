# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Business owners and teams who need a WordPress website built, redesigned, automated or made easier to find. They arrive from a search result, a referral or a profile link, and want to judge quickly whether Iqra can do the job and how to reach her.

## Product Purpose

The portfolio of Iqra Batool, a WordPress web developer. It shows what she offers, how a project runs and examples of her work, and it should end with the visitor sending a message through the contact form or by email.

## Positioning

One person covers three jobs that are usually split: WordPress and Bricks Builder development, AI solutions and automation, and SEO (including AEO and GEO). She holds an MPhil in Computer Science.

## Operating Context

A static site built by `scripts/build.js` from the data files in `content/`, deployed on Vercel. It is one landing page plus a case study page per project. There is no blog.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Sections on the landing page, in order: hero, about, services, process, work, FAQ, contact.
- Each project keeps its own case study page.
- No framework and no npm dependencies. GSAP, ScrollTrigger and Lenis load from a CDN, and the site must still read correctly without them.
- The contact form has no endpoint configured yet (`content/site.js` → `form.endpoint`). Until it is set, the form tells the visitor to email instead.
- Social links, phone and location are empty in `content/site.js` and stay hidden until filled in.

## Brand Commitments

- Palette: Burgundy Wine `#5B0F18`, Soft Cream `#F8F1E7`, Deep Black `#0B0B0B`, black-led (owner's decision, November 2026).
- wptechstudio.com is the owner's reference for animation and for how content is organised.
- Header: logo left, menu centred, "Let's Talk" button on the right as an outlined pill with an arrow circle.
- The IB monogram.

## Evidence on Hand

- Two stats: 1.5+ years of experience and 20+ projects completed.
- Four projects in `content/projects.js`, each with a summary, objective, approach and outcome. Their previews are illustrations generated by `scripts/art.js`, not screenshots.
- Four SEO questions and answers in `content/services.js`.
- There are no testimonials, client logos or pricing. Do not invent any.

## Product Principles

1. Say what is offered in plain words; no promises about rankings or AI results.
2. The work and the process are the proof.
3. Every section should make it easy to get in touch.
4. Nothing is shown that is empty or made up.
